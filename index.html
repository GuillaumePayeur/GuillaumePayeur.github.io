Guillaume Payeur

Hi! I’m Guillaume, a PhD student in physics at the Université de Montréal. I work under the supervision of Laurence Perreault-Levasseur. Previously, I received my Master’s degree from McGill University, where I worked under the supervision of Robert Brandenberger and Evan McDonough. I’m interested in the application of machine learning methods to cosmology and astrophysics. I’m also interested in the intersection of cosmology and high-energy theoretical physics.
