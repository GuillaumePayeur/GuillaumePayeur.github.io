01 / Research

Finding structure in difficult data.

My work connects physical models with modern learning systems to solve nonlinear, high-dimensional inference problems in astrophysics and cosmology.

R / 01

Strong gravitational lensing

Reconstructing the distant source and foreground mass from the distorted light we observe.

R / 02

High-dimensional inference

Moving beyond restrictive parametric models to infer rich, pixel-level posterior distributions.

R / 03

Scientific machine learning

Combining diffusion models, recurrent inference machines, and differentiable simulations.

R / 04

Fundamental cosmology

Using observations and theory to investigate dark energy, dark matter, and the early Universe.
